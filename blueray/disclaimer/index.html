Measurement & Disclaimer

BlueRay · Last updated August 9, 2026 · Contact: superdaddy2026@163.com

This page explains the limits of BlueRay’s measurements for users and App Store review.

1. What this app is

BlueRay uses the iPhone rear camera and system exposure/white-balance signals to estimate illuminance (lux), correlated color temperature (kelvin), and light flicker trends. Results are for everyday eye-care habits—not a certified meter, engineering instrument, or medical device.

2. Illuminance (lux)

  • Lux is estimated from aperture, shutter, ISO and related parameters with an empirical calibration.
  • Phone model, lens, surface reflectance, and aiming angle all affect readings.
  • Aim at a matte wall or desk, hold steady 2–3 seconds; do not aim at screens, lamp LEDs, or mirrors.
  • Changing the surface or angle can change the reading—that is expected.
  • Values may differ from a professional lux meter and must not be used for compliance, occupational hygiene, or safety certification.

3. Color temperature (kelvin)

Kelvin is estimated from camera white-balance related data and can shift with mixed lighting or colored walls. Night “cool light” tips are lifestyle guidance only.

4. Flicker

  • Observation uses a short exposure (~1/1000s) to reveal rolling-shutter stripes.
  • The percentage is an estimate from brightness variation.
  • Results depend on frame rate, lamp type, distance, hand shake, and sensor. They cannot replace a flicker meter, lamp test report, or medical/safety advice.

5. Disclaimer

  • Readings, guidance, and eye-care reminders are for personal everyday reference only.
  • The developer is not liable for losses from relying solely on this app.
  • Seek an eye-care professional if you have vision or eye-health symptoms.
